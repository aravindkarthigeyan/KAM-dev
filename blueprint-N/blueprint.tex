\author{Scott Armstrong and Zohar Komargodski}

\begin{abstract}
A proof plan, step by step, for the three frozen statements of track N of the KAM formalization:
the exponential stability estimate for strictly quasi-convex nearly integrable Hamiltonians with a
complex Hessian bound (N-HEAD-C, after P\"oschel 1993, Theorem~1), the exact integrable dynamics for
zero perturbation (N-ZERO) and the nonsingular isoenergetic derivative (N-QC). Every result is
labelled and cites its pinned source. This version contains Part~0 only; the plan of the remaining
parts is in \texttt{PLAN.md}.
\end{abstract}
