## Method and limits

Collected candidates from track I/N/P notes, statements, source/audit/response packets and all blueprint part/review errata and source-gap inventories. Reconciled later independent rejections with the writer allegations. In particular read the required P `p11-route-study.md`, `p11a-russmann-check.md` and N `p11-review-1.md`; their findings are mapped below rather than treated as automatic proof authority. Source error claims were checked against the pinned pages. Formula transcriptions in the ledger use Unicode mathematical notation and omit irrelevant surrounding text; an ellipsis separates quoted excerpts rather than supplying unprinted words. Printed-page locators govern.

Fresh page-separated PDF extraction and, where the text layer was inadequate, rendered scans were used. Critical source coverage included HL19 pp.18–20,46,49–53,59; Chi05 pp.8–17; CC03 p.7; BM11 pp.4–5,8–9,12–13; GCB16 pp.3–4; Pos93 printed pp.190,194–195,197–200,202–204,214 and the relevant appendix interfaces; CP10 pp.554–566,569–574 with its definition/measure context; CP11 pp.21,34,62–63; revised Féjoz pp.17,33–35,38–39,42,44–48,50–58; the five-page Féjoz erratum; all four original Nei76 pages; and the Nek77/LP17 pages identified in the existing D25 entries. This is targeted verification of the recorded assertions, not an audit of every proof in those papers.

Classifications distinguish a false assertion or misprint from a supplied proof of a true step. A source import not justified by the literal hypotheses is recorded with its exact scope; this does not refute the source headline theorem. Where only a proof mismatch or ambiguous interpretation was established, the CANDIDATE and `checked_by` explicitly retain UNVERIFIED status for the mathematical allegation. ADOPTED means a frozen/owner-approved convention was selected, not that its provider is proved. PLANNED means a blueprint/draft route or supplement remains to be implemented and checked.
