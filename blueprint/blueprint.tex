\begin{abstract}
A complete proof plan, step by step, for the five frozen statements of the KAM formalization:
the positive-measure KAM theorem with rate $\varepsilon^{1/4}$ and its qualitative form, the
a-posteriori KAM theorem at fixed frequency, local uniqueness of invariant tori of flows, and the
measure of the non-Diophantine set. Every result is labelled and cites its pinned source; the
dependency graph of the formalization is extracted from this document.
\end{abstract}
