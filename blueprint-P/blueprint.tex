\begin{abstract}
A proof plan, step by step, for the three frozen statements of track P: the properly degenerate KAM
theorem of Chierchia--Pinzari (specialized as frozen), the analytic planar two-planet theorem with a
moving Sun, and the fixed-Sun $n$-planet variant. Part~0 fixes conventions, restates every frozen
definition and the three frozen theorems; the remaining parts are listed in \texttt{PLAN.md}.
Results of track K's blueprint are cited as \textup{[K:\texttt{label}]}.
\end{abstract}
