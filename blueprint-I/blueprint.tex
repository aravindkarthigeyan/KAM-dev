\begin{abstract}
A proof plan for the four frozen statements of track~I: the isoenergetic a-posteriori KAM theorem
(I-G1), all-time action confinement for two degrees of freedom (I-HEAD), the Kepler bordered
determinant (I-KEPLER), and the near-circular region of the restricted circular planar three-body
problem (I-R3BP). Results of the blueprint of track~K are cited by their labels \Kref{bp:p1:\dots}
and \Kref{bp:p2:\dots}. Every result is labelled and cites its pinned source.
\end{abstract}
